description: Review the week in QTR and set up the next one
---

Run the user's weekly review in QTR, using the qtr skill.

1. Call `get_week_review`.
2. Share what got done, what slipped, the goals that need a look and why, and where the quarter stands.
3. Ask what they want next week to be about, then offer `save_week_plan` for it and `reschedule_overdue` for what slipped. Run each only once they say yes.
