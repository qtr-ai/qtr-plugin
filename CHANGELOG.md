# Changelog

The QTR connector reads the newest entry and shows it to people on an older version of the skill, so write each entry for them: what they can do now, in a line or two.

## 1.1.2 (2026-11-02)

- Claude uses the desk's words when it arranges your cards: tile and untile, as in the QTR app's menus.

## 1.1.1 (2026-11-02)

- Work in your team workspaces from Claude: say which one ("my tasks in Qtr Connect") and Claude reads, adds and plans there.
- The connector's address is now `https://api.qtr.ai/mcp/sse`. In Claude Code, sign in to QTR once more after updating.

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-01)

- In Claude, your tasks, time blocks, a goal's map, Ansel's waiting changes, your daily brief, the quarter and the focus clock now show in the chat, drawn like your QTR desk, and you can work in them there.
- Add several tasks in one go, read any search result in full, and use the new /qtr: commands in Claude Code.

## 1.0.1 (2026-11-01)

- Free QTR accounts can connect now, not only Pro.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-01)

- First release: the QTR connector and the `qtr` skill, with every tool the connector offers.
